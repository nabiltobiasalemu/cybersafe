Common Types of Cyber Threats

Cyber threats are dangers that can affect our devices, personal information and online accounts. Here are some common examples:

1. Phishing

Phishing is when attackers pretend to be trusted organizations or people to steal personal information through fake emails, messages or websites.

2. Malware

Malware is harmful software designed to damage devices, steal information or gain unauthorized access.

3. Ransomware

Ransomware is a type of malware that locks or encrypts files and demands money to restore access.

4. Social Engineering

Social engineering is when attackers manipulate people into revealing private information or doing something that compromises their security.

How to Recognize Suspicious Activity

  • Unexpected messages asking for passwords.
  • Unknown or suspicious links.
  • Messages creating a sense of urgency.
  • Unusual activity on your accounts.
