What is cybersecurity?

Cybersecurity is the practice of protecting people, systems and data from cyberattacks by using various technologies, processes and policies.

Why is cybersecurity important?

Cybersecurity is important because it helps protect sensitive information and critical infrastructure from cyber threats. As our lives become more dependent on digital technologies, the risks of cyberattacks increase, making it essential to have robust security measures in place.
